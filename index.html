About Me

I’m an undergraduate electrical engineering student at Harvard University who enjoys turning ideas into tangible hardware and working systems. My interests span analog circuit design, embedded systems, optical engineering, computer architecture, robotics, and electronic devices.

I enjoy bridging theory and practice through hands-on work with circuits, signals, PCBs, sensors, microcontrollers, optical instrumentation, and FPGA-based systems. I’m especially motivated by problems that require both careful analysis and practical implementation.

My experience includes designing PCBs and analog circuits, creating embedded sensing systems, building optical measurement setups, implementing digital hardware in SystemVerilog, and developing Python-based tools for engineering analysis and automation.

I’m currently interested in engineering opportunities where I can contribute to hardware, embedded, optical, or electrical systems while continuing to learn from experienced engineers and researchers. I’m always open to discussing technical projects, research, internships, and future collaborations.
