Miniature Basketball Robot

Project Overview

Objective

Design and build a rover-style robot to compete in a basketball-themed engineering competition. The robot was required to collect tennis balls and accurately shoot them through a basketball hoop on uneven terrain.

Details

  • Designed and constructed a rover from acrylic, wood, 3D-printed components, metal rods, gears, and provided DC motors
  • Modeled custom wheel molds in SolidWorks and fabricated them using a CNC mill
  • Designed a motor-driven lifting arm powered by a DC motor, gearbox, and gear train
  • Built a rigid acrylic chassis and implemented a drivetrain powered by brushless DC motors

Responsibilities

  • Collaborated in a four-person team to define project goals, design the robot architecture, and compete in the tournament
  • Led the mechanical design of the arm mechanism, including gearbox and gear train design in SolidWorks
  • Machined and assembled the arm components
  • Contributed significantly to final robot assembly, including chassis construction and arm integration

Outcome

Achieved 2nd place out of 16 teams in the Introductory Mechanical Engineering tournament, competing in head-to-head one-on-one matches.
