Miniature Basketball Robot

Project Overview

Objective

Design and build a rover-style robot to compete in a basketball-themed engineering competition, requiring collection of tennis balls and accurate shooting through a basketball hoop on uneven terrain.

Details

  • Designed and constructed a rover from scratch using acrylic, wood, 3D-printed components, metal rods, gears, and provided DC motors
  • Modeled custom wheel molds in SolidWorks and fabricated them using a CNC mill
  • Designed a motor-driven lifting arm powered by a DC motor with a gearbox and gear train to raise and position tennis balls
  • Built a rigid acrylic chassis and implemented a drivetrain powered by brushless DC motors

Responsibilities

  • Collaborated in a four-person team to define project goals, design the robot architecture, and compete in the tournament
  • Led the mechanical design of the arm mechanism, including gearbox and gear train design in SolidWorks
  • Machined and assembled the arm components and contributed significantly to final robot assembly, including chassis construction and arm integration

Outcome

Achieved 2nd place out of 16 teams in the Introductory Mechanical Engineering tournament, competing in head-to-head (1v1) matches.
