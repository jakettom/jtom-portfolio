Mars Rover — Robotics Club

Project Overview

Objective

Contribute to the electrical design and system integration of a student-built rover for the University Rover Challenge, with a particular focus on analog circuit design and implementation.

Details

  • Collaborated with multidisciplinary teams across science, mechanical, software, and business groups
  • Currently designing an integrated sensor board for rover science objectives
  • Assisted in designing a power distribution board using KiCad
  • Supported the creation and organization of a full rover-level electrical schematic

Responsibilities

  • Currently leading testing and Arduino firmware implementation for a science sensor module
  • Contributed to schematic implementation of buck converters, fuses, and WAGO connectors
  • Helped integrate electrical subsystems into a coherent rover circuit diagram
  • Participated in design reviews, iteration, and electrical system debugging

Tools & Software

  • KiCad for schematic capture and PCB design
  • Power electronics components
  • Buck converters and fuses
  • WAGO electrical connectors
  • Arduino firmware development

Outcome

Contributed to a functional power distribution board and organized rover-wide electrical schematics that supported successful system integration and testing.
