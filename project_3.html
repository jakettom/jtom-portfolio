Project Overview

Objective

Contribute to the electrical design and system integration of a student-built rover for the University Rover Challenge. Particularly focused on analog circuit design and implementaiton.

Details

  • Collaborated with multidisciplinary teams to develop a competition rover (science, mechanical, software, business).
  • (Currently) Designing an integrated sensor board for rover science objectives
  • Assisted in the design of a power distribution board (PDB) using KiCad
  • Supported creation and organization of a full rover-level electrical schematic

Responsibilities

  • (Currently) Independently leading testing and Arduino firmware implementation of science sensor module
  • Contributed to schematic implementation of buck converters, fuses, and WAGO connectors
  • Helped integrate electrical subsystems into a coherent rover circuit diagram
  • Participated in design reviews, iteration, and electrical system debugging

Tools & Software

  • KiCad (schematic capture and PCB design)
  • Power electronics components (buck converters, fuses)
  • Electrical connectors (WAGO)

Outcome

Contributed to a fully functional power distribution board and organized rover-wide electrical schematics that supported successful system integration and testing.
